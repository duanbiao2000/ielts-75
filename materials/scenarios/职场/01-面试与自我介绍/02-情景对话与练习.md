# 面试与自我介绍 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：面试开场与自我介绍

**Interviewer**: Good morning. Thanks for coming in. Could you start by telling me a little about yourself?

**You**: **【Good morning. It's a pleasure to meet you. / Good morning, thank you for having me.】** My name is **【…】**. I'm currently **【a data analyst with three years of experience / working as a data analyst, and I've been doing it for three years】**.

**Interviewer**: And what would you say is your greatest strength?

**You**: **【I'd say my greatest strength is being detail-oriented and reliable. / I think my main strength is my attention to detail.】** For example, **【in my current role, I'm responsible for analysing data, and accuracy is essential / I handle data analysis, where accuracy really matters】**.

**Interviewer**: Why are you interested in this position?

**You**: **【What attracts me most is the chance to grow in a company with a strong reputation. / I'm applying for this role because it offers both growth and the chance to work on challenging projects.】** I believe **【my experience fits this role well, because / my background matches what you're looking for, since】** I've been doing similar work for three years.

## 对话二：收尾与提问

**Interviewer**: Do you have any questions for us?

**You**: **【Yes, I'd like to know more about the team I'd be working with. / I'd like to ask about the team structure.】**

**Interviewer**: Sure, the team is currently …

**You**: Thank you, that's very helpful. **【Thank you for your time today. I'm looking forward to hearing from you. / Thanks again for your time — I look forward to your reply.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，完整模拟面试（自我介绍 → 3 个常见问题 → 提问 → 收尾），角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人录制一段 90 秒自我介绍，回听检查结构（背景 → 经验 → 契合点）与流畅度；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 自我介绍是否控制在 1–2 分钟且有重点？
   - 谈优势时是否有具体事例？
   - 收尾提问是否得体？
4. **迁移练习**：把自我介绍结构与话题库「工作与职业」话题卡结合，
   录一段"描述你的工作"的回答。
