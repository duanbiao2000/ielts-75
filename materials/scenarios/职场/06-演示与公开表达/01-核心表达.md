# 演示与公开表达 · 核心表达

## 一、场景高频词

**结构与呈现**：presentation, demo, agenda, highlight, takeaway,
overview, walk through, in summary

**控场与互动**：audience, Q&A, point out, emphasize, clarify,
for instance, what this means is

## 二、"开口就说"基础句型

**开场**
- Today I'd like to walk you through …（今天我想带大家了解…）
- Let me start with the big picture, and then go into the details.（我先讲整体，再深入细节）
- The main purpose of this session is to …（这次分享的主要目的是…）

**展开**
- There are three points I'd like to cover.（我想讲三点）
- First of all, … / Next, … / Finally, …（首先…/其次…/最后…）
- What this means is that …（这意味着…）
- Take … for instance.（以…为例）

**强调与收束**
- The key takeaway here is …（这里的关键是…）
- What matters most is …（最重要的是…）
- To sum up, … / In summary, …（总结一下，…）

## 三、进阶表达

- **设问开场**：So what happens when …? Let me show you what we found.
- **过渡信号**：Let's move on to the next point. / This brings me to my second point.
- **Q&A 应对**：That's a great question. The short answer is … / Let me clarify — …
- **坦诚未知**：I don't have the exact figures at hand, but I'll follow up with you later.
- **引导互动**：I'd be happy to take any questions at the end. / Feel free to interrupt.

## 四、易错提醒

- **别照读幻灯片**：口头表达要口语化，把书面句子改成口语（书面语 → 口语转换）；
- **结构要"说"出来**：用过渡句让听众跟上（First / Next / Finally），不要只靠 PPT 顺序；
- **别堆术语**：听众不一定懂行业术语，必要时用一句话解释；
- **Q&A 别急着答**：先复述确认问题（If I understand correctly, you're asking…），再回答；
- **控制语速**：公开场合容易越讲越快，刻意放慢，重点处停顿。
