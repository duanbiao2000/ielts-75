# 客户沟通与商务洽谈 · 核心表达

## 一、场景高频词

**沟通与洽谈**：client, negotiation, proposal, quotation, contract,
deadline, terms, partnership, objection, compromise

**态度与动作**：confirm, arrange, follow up, walk through, guarantee,
be flexible, meet each other halfway

## 二、"开口就说"基础句型

**开场与定调**
- We'd like to discuss the details of the proposal.（我们希望讨论一下方案细节）
- Thank you for taking the time to meet with us today.（感谢您今天抽时间与我们会面）
- Let's start by going over what we've prepared.（我们先过一遍我们准备的内容）

**说明立场与需求**
- What we're looking for is …（我们想要的是…）
- Our main concern is the timeline.（我们最关心的是时间线）
- We're prepared to move forward if we can agree on …（如果能就…达成一致，我们可以推进）

**回应与协商**
- Could you walk us through your pricing?（能给我们介绍一下你们的报价吗？）
- That works for us, provided that …（这我们可以接受，前提是…）
- We might be able to offer … in exchange for …（我们也许可以提供…以换取…）

## 三、进阶表达

- **表达立场**：We're of the view that … / Our position is that …
- **委婉异议**：We understand your concern, but we'd like to point out that …
- **让步与条件**：If we could …, we'd be able to … / We're willing to … as long as …
- **交还话语权**：What are your thoughts on this? / How does that sound from your side?
- **收尾确认**：To confirm, we've agreed on … / I'll follow up with you by Friday.

## 四、易错提醒

- **语气别太硬**：直接说 "No, that's not acceptable" 会破坏关系，
  用 We're not able to… / What we can do is… 替代；
- **让步别无底线**：让步要带条件（provided that / as long as），否则显得没有谈判空间；
- **别一味指责对方**：处理异议时先认可（I understand your concern），再给理由；
- **"洽谈"不是"聊天"**：语域偏正式，但不要书面腔，保持口语化的正式。
