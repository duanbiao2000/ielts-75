# 餐饮与点餐 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：餐厅点餐

**You**: Excuse me, **【a table for two, please. / could we have a table for two?】**

**Waiter**: Of course. Here are your menus.

**You**: Thank you. **【Could you recommend anything? / Do you have any recommendations?】**

**Waiter**: The grilled fish is very popular.

**You**: Sounds good. **【I'll go with the grilled fish. / I'd like the grilled fish.】** And could I have **【a side of vegetables / some rice】** with it?

**Waiter**: Certainly. Anything to drink?

**You**: Just **【a glass of water, please. / water, please.】**

## 对话二：特殊需求与反馈

**You**: Hi, I'd like to order the pasta. **【I'm allergic to nuts — could you make sure it doesn't contain any? / I'm allergic to nuts; is it possible to avoid them?】**

**Waiter**: Of course, I'll note that for the kitchen.

**You**: Thank you. **【Also, could you make it mild? / Could you keep it not too spicy?】**

**(用餐后)**

**You**: The pasta was delicious, thank you. **【It was one of the best meals I've had. / It was really nice.】**

**Waiter**: I'm glad you enjoyed it.

**You**: **【Could we have the bill, please? / Could we get the check?】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，分别扮演顾客与服务员，完整走一遍"入座 → 推荐 → 点餐 → 特殊需求 → 结账"；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意礼貌语气与问句语调；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否流畅完成点餐全流程？
   - 能否准确表达过敏、口味等特殊需求？
   - 反馈时是否礼貌且明确？
4. **迁移练习**：把点餐表达与话题库「健康与生活方式」结合，
   录一段"我最喜欢的餐厅/一道菜"的回答。
