# 租房与居住沟通 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：看房咨询

**You**: Hi, I'm **【looking for a one-bedroom flat near the university. / looking for a flat close to the campus.】** Do you have anything available?

**Agent**: Yes, I have a nice one-bedroom flat on the third floor. The rent is 800 a month.

**You**: That sounds good. **【Could I take a look at it? / Is it possible to view the flat?】** And I was wondering — **【what's included in the rent? / does the rent include utilities?】**

**Agent**: Sure. The rent includes water and electricity, but you'll need to pay for the internet.

**You**: I see. **【What about the deposit? / How much is the deposit?】**

**Agent**: One month's rent, and it's refundable at the end of the lease.

**You**: Great, that works for me. **【When can I move in? / When would the flat be available?】**

## 对话二：入住后反馈问题

**You**: Hi, I'd like to report a problem with the **【heating / air conditioner】** in my flat. **【It's not working properly. / It hasn't been working properly for a few days.】**

**Landlord**: I'm sorry about that. I'll arrange for someone to fix it.

**You**: Thank you. **【Could you arrange to fix it as soon as possible? / Would it be possible to send someone soon?】** It's getting quite cold at night, so I'm a bit concerned.

**Landlord**: No problem, I'll send a technician tomorrow.

**You**: That's great, thank you for your help.

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，分别扮演租客与房东/中介，完整走一遍"看房 → 问细节 → 入住 → 反馈问题"；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成对话一和对话二，注意问句的语调（升调）；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否流畅问清租金、包含项目、押金、租期四大细节？
   - 投诉时是否礼貌且明确？
   - 能否用 would it be possible / I was wondering if 等委婉句式？
4. **迁移练习**：把租房句型与话题库「住宿与家乡」Part 1 结合，录音回答相关问题。
