# 银行与电话客服 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：致电银行客服

**Agent**: Thank you for calling the bank. How can I help you?

**You**: Hello, **【I'm calling about my account. / I'd like some help with my account.】** I've noticed an **【unusual transaction / a charge I don't recognise】**, and I'd like you to **【look into it / check it for me】**.

**Agent**: I'm sorry to hear that. Could you confirm your account number and name, please?

**You**: Sure, my account number is **【1234567890】**, and my name is **【…】**.

**Agent**: Thank you. I can see the transaction. It appears to be **【a duplicate charge / a charge from a merchant】**. I'll raise a dispute for you.

**You**: Thank you. **【When can I expect a response? / How long will it take to resolve?】** And **【could you give me a reference number? / is there a reference number I can note?】**

**Agent**: It usually takes 3–5 business days. Your reference number is **【…】**.

**You**: Perfect, thank you for your help.

## 对话二：办理业务

**You**: Hi, **【I'd like to open a bank account. / I'd like to open an account.】** What documents do I need?

**Agent**: You'll need your ID, proof of address, and a reference number.

**You**: I have my ID and **【a utility bill / proof of address】**. **【Is there anything else I need to do? / Anything else I should prepare?】**

**Agent**: That's all for the application. The account will be active within a week.

**You**: Great, thank you. **【Is there a minimum balance requirement? / Do I need to keep a minimum balance?】**

**Agent**: No, there isn't.

**You**: Perfect, that's very helpful.

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，分别扮演客户与客服，完成"异常交易投诉"与"开户"两段对话；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意电话用语的正式度与礼貌度；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否在电话中清晰说明问题并确认信息？
   - 能否主动索要工单号与回复时间？
   - 语气是否礼貌且不卑不亢？
4. **迁移练习**：把客服沟通表达与话题库「社会融入与社区」结合，
   录一段"我处理过的一个服务问题"的回答。
