# 医疗与就诊 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：预约与描述症状

**You**: Hello, **【I'd like to make an appointment with the doctor. / I'd like to book an appointment with a doctor.】**

**Receptionist**: Sure. What seems to be the problem?

**You**: **【I've had a headache and a sore throat for about three days. / I've had a headache for three days, and my throat hurts too.】** It started **【a few days ago / on Monday】**, and it's getting **【a bit worse / more uncomfortable】**.

**Receptionist**: Let me check the schedule. How about tomorrow at 10 a.m.?

**You**: That works for me. **【Thank you. / That's fine.】**

## 对话二：看医生

**Doctor**: What brings you here today?

**You**: **【I've come to see you because I've been feeling unwell for a few days. / I'm not feeling well — it's been going on for a few days.】** I've had a **【cough and a fever / headache and dizziness】**, and I've been taking **【some medicine / paracetamol】**, but it hasn't helped much.

**Doctor**: Let me take a look. Based on the symptoms, it's probably **【a cold / a minor infection】**.

**You**: **【What should I do? / Is there anything I can do?】**

**Doctor**: I'll prescribe some medicine. **【Take it twice a day after meals, and get plenty of rest. / Take it twice a day with food, and rest as much as you can.】**

**You**: **【Are there any side effects? / Is there anything I should watch out for?】** And when should I come back?

**Doctor**: The side effects are minor. If you're not better in three days, come back for a follow-up.

**You**: Thank you, doctor.

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，分别扮演病人与医生/前台，走完"预约 → 就诊 → 医嘱"全流程；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意描述症状时的逻辑（部位 → 感受 → 时长）；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否准确描述症状的部位、感受、持续时间？
   - 能否礼貌追问医嘱细节？
   - 时态是否正确（描述症状用现在完成时/现在时）？
4. **迁移练习**：把症状描述与话题库「健康与生活方式」结合，
   录一段"我如何保持健康"的回答。
