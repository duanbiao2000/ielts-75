# 聚会邀请与回应 · 核心表达

## 一、发出邀请

**邀请**
- **【I'd like to invite you to … / I'd love to have you at …】**（我想邀请你参加…）
- **【Would you like to come to …? / Do you want to join us for …?】**（你想来…吗？）
- **【Are you free on …? We're having … / Are you free on …? We're planning …】**（…有空吗？我们打算…）

**确认细节**
- **【The party is at … on … / It's at … on …】**（聚会在…（时间）于…（地点）举行）
- **【Let me know if you can make it. / Let me know if you're able to come.】**（告诉我你能否来）

## 二、接受邀请

- **【I'd love to — thanks for inviting me. / I'd love to, thanks for the invite.】**（我很乐意——谢谢邀请）
- **【That sounds great — I'll be there. / That sounds wonderful, I'll definitely come.】**（听起来很棒，我会去）
- **【Yes, I'm free on … — count me in. / Yes, I'm free that day — count me in.】**（…我有空——算我一个）

## 三、婉拒邀请

**委婉拒绝**
- **【I'd love to, but I'm afraid I can't make it. / I'd love to, but I can't make it.】**（我很想去，但恐怕来不了）
- **【I'm sorry, but I already have plans on … / Sorry, I've already got plans that day.】**（抱歉，那天我已经有安排了）
- **【I'd have to check my schedule — let me get back to you. / Let me check my schedule and get back to you.】**（我需要看下日程，稍后回复你）

**表达遗憾与替代方案**
- **【I'm really sorry to miss it — hope it goes well. / I'm sorry I can't make it — hope it's a great time.】**（很遗憾不能参加，希望聚会顺利）
- **【Maybe next time — let's catch up soon. / Perhaps next time — we should catch up soon.】**（下次吧——我们找时间聚聚）

## 四、高频句型

- Would you like to …?（你想…吗？）
- I'd love to, but …（我很想去，但…）
- Let me know if you can make it.（告诉我你能否来）
- Count me in.（算我一个）

## 五、易错提醒

- 婉拒先表达意愿或感谢（I'd love to, but…），再说明原因，不伤关系；
- 拒绝后表达遗憾（hope it goes well），体现礼貌；
- 邀请时给出时间、地点等具体细节，便于对方决定。
