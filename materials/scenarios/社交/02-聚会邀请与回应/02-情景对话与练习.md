# 聚会邀请与回应 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：发出邀请

**You**: **【Hi, we're having a small dinner party this Saturday — would you like to come? / Hi, we're planning a small dinner party on Saturday — would you like to join?】**

**Friend**: **【That sounds great — I'd love to. / That sounds wonderful, I'd love to come.】**

**You**: **【Great — it's at my place at 7 p.m. / Perfect — it starts at seven at my place.】** **【Let me know if you can make it. / Just let me know if you're still able to come.】**

**Friend**: **【I'll be there — count me in. / I'll definitely be there.】**

**You**: **【Perfect, see you then. / Great, looking forward to it.】**

## 对话二：婉拒邀请

**You**: **【I'd love to join the trip, but I'm afraid I can't make it. / I'd really like to come on the trip, but I can't make it.】** **【I already have plans that weekend. / I've already got plans that weekend.】**

**Friend**: No problem.

**You**: **【I'm really sorry to miss it — hope it goes well. / I'm sorry I can't join — I hope it's a great trip.】** **【Maybe next time — let's catch up soon. / Perhaps next time — we should definitely catch up soon.】**

**Friend**: **【Definitely, we'll plan something else. / Sure, we'll arrange something else.】**

**You**: **【Thanks for understanding. / Thanks for being so understanding.】**

## 对话三：确认与婉拒（时间未定）

**You**: **【I'd love to come, but I'm not sure about Friday — let me check my schedule and get back to you. / I'd love to, but I'm not sure about Friday — let me check and confirm.】**

**Friend**: Sure, no rush.

**You**: **【I'll let you know by tomorrow. / I'll get back to you by tomorrow.】** **【Thanks again for the invite. / Thanks again for inviting me.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，完成"邀请-接受""邀请-婉拒""确认-待定"三段对话，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成三段对话，注意婉拒的礼貌程度；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 邀请时能否给出时间、地点等具体细节？
   - 婉拒时能否先表意愿/感谢，再说明原因？
   - 能否表达遗憾并给出替代方案？
4. **迁移练习**：把邀请表达与话题库「社会融入与社区」结合，
   录一段"描述一次你参加的社区活动"的回答。
