# 日常寒暄与介绍 · 核心表达

## 一、问候与寒暄

**打招呼**
- **【How are you? / How's it going?】**（你好吗？/ 最近怎么样？）
- **【Long time no see! / It's been a while!】**（好久不见！）
- **【What have you been up to? / What have you been doing lately?】**（最近在忙什么？）

**回应**
- **【I've been keeping busy with … / I've been busy with …】**（我最近在忙…）
- **【Nothing much — just … / Not much, just …】**（没什么特别的——就是…）
- **【It's going well, thanks. / Everything's good, thanks.】**（一切都好，谢谢）

**天气与近况（经典破冰）**
- **【Lovely day, isn't it? / Nice weather we're having.】**（天气真好，对吧？）
- **【How's your day going? / Having a good day?】**（今天过得怎么样？）

## 二、介绍表达

**自我介绍**
- **【Hi, I'm … — nice to meet you. / Hi, my name is … — nice to meet you.】**
- **【I'm … — I work / study at …】**（我是…，在…工作/学习）

**介绍他人**
- **【This is … — we met at … / Let me introduce …】**（这是…，我们在…认识的）
- **【I'd like you to meet … / Come and meet …】**（我想让你认识…/过来见见…）

**被介绍时**
- **【Nice to meet you. / It's a pleasure to meet you.】**（很高兴认识您）
- **【I've heard a lot about you. / I've heard so much about you.】**（久仰大名）

## 三、闲聊延续

- **【So, what do you do? / What line of work are you in?】**（你是做什么的？）
- **【How do you know …? / How did you meet …?】**（你怎么认识…的？）
- **【Do you often come here? / Are you a regular here?】**（你常来这里吗？）
- **【That sounds interesting — tell me more. / That sounds interesting, what's it like?】**（听起来很有趣——多讲讲）

## 四、高频句型

- How are you doing? / What have you been up to?（你好吗/最近忙什么）
- Nice to meet you.（很高兴认识你）
- I'd like you to meet …（我想让你认识…）
- That sounds interesting — tell me more.（听起来很有趣，多讲讲）

## 五、易错提醒

- 寒暄后要接一句回应（I've been keeping busy with…），避免只问不答；
- 介绍他人用 This is…，不用 He is… 直接介绍（更自然）；
- 闲聊话题从安全话题切入（工作、近况、共同环境），避免隐私。
