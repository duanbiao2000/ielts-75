# 日常寒暄与介绍 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：偶遇寒暄

**You**: **【Hi! Long time no see. / Hey! It's been a while.】** **【How have you been? / How are you doing?】**

**Friend**: **【I've been keeping busy with work, but it's going well. / I've been busy with work, but everything's good.】** **【What about you? / How about you?】**

**You**: **【Not much — just finishing up a project. / Nothing much, just wrapping up a project.】** **【How's your day going? / Having a good day?】**

**Friend**: **【Pretty good, thanks. / It's going well, thanks.】**

**You**: **【Glad to hear that. Let's catch up properly sometime. / Good to hear — we should catch up properly sometime.】**

**Friend**: **【Definitely. / Sounds good.】**

## 对话二：介绍新朋友

**You**: **【Hi, I'm … — nice to meet you. / Hi, my name is … — it's a pleasure to meet you.】**

**New person**: **【Nice to meet you too. / You too.】**

**You**: **【I'm a student at the university — what about you? / I study at the university — what about you?】**

**New person**: **【I work at a tech company nearby. / I work for a tech company just nearby.】**

**You**: **【That sounds interesting — tell me more. / That sounds interesting — what's it like?】**

**New person**: **【It's quite fast-paced, but I enjoy it. / It's fast-paced, but I really enjoy it.】**

**You**: **【I'd like to hear more about it — maybe we can grab a coffee later. / I'd love to hear more — maybe we can get a coffee later.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟偶遇寒暄与介绍新朋友，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意寒暄的自然语调；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否用多种方式问候并回应近况？
   - 介绍他人与自我介绍是否得体自然？
   - 能否延续话题、表达兴趣（That sounds interesting）？
4. **迁移练习**：把寒暄表达与话题库「人物与关系」结合，
   录一段"描述一个你新认识的人"的回答。
