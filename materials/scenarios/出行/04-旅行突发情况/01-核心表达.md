# 旅行突发情况 · 核心表达

## 一、求助开场

- Excuse me, could you help me, please?（打扰一下，能帮我一下吗？）
- I seem to have **【gotten lost / lost my way】**.（我好像迷路了）
- I need some help with …（我需要帮助处理…）

## 二、描述问题

**迷路**
- I'm trying to find **【my hotel / the station】**, but I'm lost.（我想找…，但迷路了）
- **【Could you point me in the right direction? / Could you show me the right way?】**

**物品丢失**
- **【I've lost my wallet / my passport.】**（我的钱包/护照丢了）
- **【I've left my bag on the train. / I left my bag on the train.】**（我把包落在火车上了）
- **【Could you help me report it? / Could you help me report the loss?】**

**身体不适**
- **【I'm not feeling very well. / I feel unwell.】**
- **【I need to see a doctor / find a hospital.】**（我需要看医生/找医院）
- **【Could you help me call a taxi? / Could you call a taxi for me?】**

**行程变更**
- **【I've missed my train / flight.】**（我错过了火车/航班）
- **【My plans have changed, so I need to …】**（我的计划变了，需要…）

## 三、请求与应对

- **【Is there any way I can …? / Is it possible to …?】**（有什么办法能…吗？）
- **【What should I do in this situation? / What can I do?】**（这种情况我该怎么办？）
- **【Could you recommend what to do next? / Could you advise me on what to do?】**
- **【Thank you — your help is greatly appreciated. / Thank you so much for your help.】**

## 四、高频句型

- Excuse me, could you help me …?（打扰一下，能帮我…吗？）
- I seem to have …（我好像…了）
- I've lost / I've left …（我丢了/落下了…）
- What should I do?（我该怎么办？）

## 五、易错提醒

- 突发情况先说明"发生了什么"，再提具体请求，逻辑清晰；
- 保持冷静礼貌，用 could 而非直接命令；
- 描述丢失用现在完成时（I've lost），描述具体行为用一般过去时（I left it on…）。
