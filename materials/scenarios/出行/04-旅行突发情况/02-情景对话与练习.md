# 旅行突发情况 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：迷路求助

**You**: Excuse me, **【could you help me, please? I'm lost. / excuse me, I seem to have gotten lost.】**

**Local**: Of course. Where are you trying to go?

**You**: **【I'm trying to find the Royal Hotel. / I'm looking for the Royal Hotel.】** **【Could you point me in the right direction? / Could you tell me how to get there?】**

**Local**: **【Go straight ahead for two blocks, then turn right. The hotel is on your left. / Go straight on for two blocks and turn right — the hotel will be on your left.】**

**You**: **【Thank you so much. / That's very helpful, thank you.】**

## 对话二：物品丢失与求助

**You**: Excuse me, **【I've lost my wallet. / I've lost my wallet and I'm not sure what to do.】** **【Could you help me report it? / Could you help me report the loss?】**

**Staff**: I'm sorry to hear that. When did you last see it?

**You**: **【I think I lost it on the bus about an hour ago. / I believe I left it on the bus an hour ago.】** **【What should I do in this situation? / What would you suggest I do?】**

**Staff**: **【I'd suggest calling the bus company first — they may have found it. / You could call the bus company; they often recover lost items.】** If it's not there, you can report it to the police.

**You**: **【Thank you — that's very helpful. / Thanks a lot, that's very useful.】** **【Is there anything else I should do? / Anything else I need to do?】**

**Staff**: Just keep your receipts and note down the report number.

**You**: **【I'll do that. Thank you for your help. / I'll do that — thank you.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟迷路与物品丢失两段求助，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意先陈述问题、再提请求的逻辑；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否在突发情况中冷静、清晰地描述问题？
   - 求助时能否礼貌且具体？
   - 能否用 What should I do / Is it possible to 等句型寻求解决方案？
4. **迁移练习**：把求助表达与话题库「经历与故事」结合，
   录一段"旅行中遇到困难并解决"的回答。
