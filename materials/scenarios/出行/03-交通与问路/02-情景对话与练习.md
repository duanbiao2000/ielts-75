# 交通与问路 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：问路

**You**: Excuse me, **【how can I get to the train station? / could you tell me the way to the train station?】**

**Local**: Sure. **【Go straight ahead and turn left at the second corner. / Go straight on, then take the second left.】** The station is **【on your right / on the right】**, **【next to / opposite】** the post office.

**You**: **【Is it far from here? / How far is it?】**

**Local**: **【It's about ten minutes' walk. / About a ten-minute walk.】**

**You**: **【Thank you so much. / That's very helpful, thank you.】**

## 对话二：乘车

**You**: Excuse me, **【could you tell me which bus goes to the university? / which bus goes to the university?】**

**Driver / Local**: You can take bus **【32 / thirty-two】**. **【It stops right outside the university. / It goes right to the university.】**

**You**: **【Where should I get on? / Where do I catch it?】**

**Local**: The stop is **【just over there / right over there】**, **【across the street / on the other side of the road】**.

**You**: **【And how much is the fare? / What's the fare?】**

**Local**: **【Two yuan / It's two yuan.】**

**You**: Thank you, **【that's very helpful. / thanks for your help.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟问路与乘车，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意方位词的准确表达；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否用多种方式问路（how can I get to / where is）？
   - 能否准确听读方位词（straight ahead, turn left, opposite, next to）？
   - 乘车时能否询问路线、票价、换乘？
4. **迁移练习**：把方位表达与话题库「住宿与家乡」地图题结合，
   录一段"描述位置与路线"的回答。
