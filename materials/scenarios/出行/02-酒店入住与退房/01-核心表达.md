# 酒店入住与退房 · 核心表达

## 一、入住

**预订与登记**
- I have a reservation under the name **【…】**.（我用…的名字预订了房间）
- I'd like to check in.（我想办理入住）
- Could I have a room on a **【higher floor / a quieter floor】**?（能安排高楼层/安静楼层的房间吗？）

**询问设施**
- What time is breakfast served?（早餐几点供应？）
- Could you tell me where the **【gym / pool / restaurant】** is?（能告诉我健身房/泳池/餐厅在哪里吗？）
- Is Wi-Fi free in the rooms?（房间的 Wi-Fi 免费吗？）

## 二、房间问题

**设施故障**
- There seems to be a problem with the **【air conditioner / Wi-Fi / lights】**.（空调/网络/灯好像有问题）
- The tap is leaking.（水龙头漏水）
- Could you send someone to fix it?（能派人来维修吗？）

**换房**
- I'm afraid the room is a bit noisy. **【Could I change to another room? / Is it possible to change rooms?】**
- The room is not quite what I expected.（房间与预期不太一样）

## 三、退房与结算

**退房**
- I'd like to check out, please.（我想办理退房）
- **【Could I have my bill, please? / Could I get the bill?】**
- Is it possible to **【stay a bit longer / have a late checkout】**?（能延迟退房吗？）

**行李与服务**
- Could I leave my luggage here for a few hours?（能寄存行李几小时吗？）
- Could you **【call a taxi / help with my bags】**?（能帮我叫车/拿行李吗？）

## 四、高频句型

- I have a reservation under …（我用…的名字预订了）
- I'd like to …（我想…）
- Could I / Could you …?（能否…？）
- I'm afraid …（恐怕…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 预订用 reservation，登记用 check in，退房用 check out，注意区分；
- 描述房间问题用 There seems to be a problem with…，礼貌且清晰；
- 退房前确认账单明细（Could I check the bill?）。
