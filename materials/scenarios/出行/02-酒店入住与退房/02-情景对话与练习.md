# 酒店入住与退房 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：入住

**You**: Hi, **【I have a reservation under the name … / I've booked a room under the name …】**.

**Receptionist**: Let me check. Yes, a standard room for two nights. Could I have your passport, please?

**You**: Sure, here you go.

**Receptionist**: Thank you. **【Could I have a room on a higher floor? / Is it possible to get a room on a higher floor?】**

**You**: **【Yes, if available. / Sure, if there's one available.】** And **【what time is breakfast served? / when is breakfast?】**

**Receptionist**: Breakfast is from **【7 to 10 / seven to ten】**. Here's your key card — your room is **【305 / three oh five】**.

**You**: Thank you, **【that's very helpful. / thanks a lot.】**

## 对话二：房间问题与退房

**You**: Hi, **【there seems to be a problem with the air conditioner — it's not working. / I'm afraid the air conditioner isn't working.】** **【Could you send someone to fix it? / Could you get it fixed?】**

**Receptionist**: I'm sorry about that. I'll send someone up right away.

**You**: Thank you. (退房时) **【I'd like to check out, please. / I'd like to check out.】**

**Receptionist**: Of course. **【Could I have your key card? / May I have the key?】** Here's your bill.

**You**: **【Could I check the bill, please? / Could I go over the bill?】** ... That looks right. **【Could I leave my luggage here for a few hours? / Is it possible to leave my luggage for a few hours?】**

**Receptionist**: Certainly. We'll keep it for you.

**You**: Thank you, **【that's very helpful. / I appreciate it.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟入住与退房全流程，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意礼貌用语与问句语调；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否流畅完成入住登记与设施询问？
   - 房间出问题时能否礼貌且明确地提出？
   - 退房时能否确认账单、提出寄存行李等需求？
4. **迁移练习**：把酒店表达与话题库「地点与空间」结合，
   录一段"描述一个你喜欢的住宿地方"的回答。
