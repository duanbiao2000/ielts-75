# 机场与航班 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：值机

**You**: Hi, **【I'd like to check in for my flight to London. / I'd like to check in for the London flight.】**

**Agent**: May I have your passport and booking reference?

**You**: Sure, here you go. **【Could I have a window seat? / Is it possible to get a window seat?】**

**Agent**: Let me check… Yes, here's your boarding pass. Your gate is **【23 / number 23】**, and boarding starts at **【10:30 / ten thirty】**.

**You**: Thank you. **【Is my baggage within the allowance? / Does my luggage meet the allowance?】**

**Agent**: Yes, it's fine.

**You**: Perfect, **【thanks for your help. / thank you.】**

## 对话二：航班延误与改签

**You**: Excuse me, **【my flight has been delayed. What can I do? / my flight to London has been delayed — what should I do?】**

**Agent**: I'm sorry about that. The flight is delayed by **【two hours / two hours due to weather】**.

**You**: **【Could I change to a later flight? / Is it possible to rebook to a later flight?】** I have a meeting tomorrow morning.

**Agent**: Let me check… **【I can put you on the next available flight. / There's a flight leaving in three hours.】**

**You**: **【That would be great. / That works for me.】** And **【will the airline provide any compensation or accommodation? / is there any compensation or accommodation available?】**

**Agent**: Since the delay is due to weather, compensation is limited, but I can offer you a meal voucher.

**You**: **【Thank you, that's fine. / I understand, thank you.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟值机与延误改签，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意问句语调与礼貌表达；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否流畅完成值机流程（证件、座位、登机信息）？
   - 航班延误时能否清晰说明情况并寻求解决方案？
   - 能否准确读出航班号、时间等数字信息？
4. **迁移练习**：把机场表达与话题库「出行与旅行」结合，
   录一段"一次难忘的旅行"的回答，融入机场场景细节。
