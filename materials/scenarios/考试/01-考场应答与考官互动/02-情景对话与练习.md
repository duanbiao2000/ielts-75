# 考场应答与考官互动 · 情景对话与练习

> 对话为简化版考场流程，重点练习"过渡句 + 自然应答"。
> 【】内为可替换表达，可按个人习惯替换。

## 对话一：开场与 Part 1

**Examiner**: Good afternoon. Please have a seat.
**You**: Thank you. **【Good afternoon. / Good afternoon, thanks.】**

**Examiner**: Let's start with some questions about your hometown. Where is your hometown?
**You**: **【My hometown is a medium-sized city in the south. / I'm from a medium-sized city in the south.】** It's a place **【I grew up in / I've lived in all my life】**, so I know it very well.

**Examiner**: Do you like your hometown?
**You**: **【Yes, I'm quite fond of it. / Definitely, I love it.】** The main reason is that it has a **【relaxed atmosphere / friendly atmosphere】**, and I have a lot of **【memories / connections】** there.

**Examiner**: That's interesting. Has it changed much in recent years?
**You**: **【I haven't thought about that before, but I'd say yes. / That's a good point — yes, it has.】** The most noticeable change is that the city has grown much larger, with new roads and shopping centres.

## 对话二：没听清与结束

**Examiner**: Some people prefer living in the countryside. What do you think?
**You**: **【Sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you repeat that, please?】**

**Examiner**: Of course. Some people prefer living in the countryside. What's your opinion?
**You**: **【I'd say it depends on personal preferences.】** For me, I prefer the city, **【because it offers more opportunities / as cities provide more opportunities】**. **【That said, / On the other hand,】** the countryside has its own charm, such as peace and fresh air.

**Examiner**: Thank you. That's the end of the test.
**You**: **【Thank you for your time. / Thank you, it was a pleasure talking with you.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人对练，一人扮演考官，按对话流程走一遍，注意过渡句的自然使用；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人练习，自问自答 5 个问题，每个回答前加一个过渡句（That's an interesting question 等）；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 没听清时能否礼貌请求重复，且只说一次？
   - 回答前能否自然过渡，而不是直接背答案？
   - 道别是否得体、不过度？
4. **迁移练习**：把对话中的过渡句用到话题库任意一个 Part 1 文件的问题上，录音对比。
