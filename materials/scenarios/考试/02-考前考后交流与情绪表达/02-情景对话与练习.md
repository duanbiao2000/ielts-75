# 考前考后交流与情绪表达 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达，可自由替换练习。

## 对话一：考前与朋友聊天

**A**: How's your IELTS preparation going?
**You**: **【It's going okay, but I'm a bit nervous about the speaking test. / It's going well, though I feel a bit anxious about the speaking part.】**

**A**: What are you most worried about?
**You**: **【I'm worried about being nervous and losing my words in the exam. / I'm most anxious about speaking fluently under pressure.】** To deal with it, I've been **【practising with sample questions every day / doing mock tests regularly】**, and I try to remind myself to **【speak slowly and clearly / stay calm】**.

**A**: That sounds like a good plan.
**You**: **【I hope so. I've made a study plan and I'm sticking to it. / Hopefully. I've set a routine and I'm following it.】**

## 对话二：考后交流结果

**A**: How did the exam go?
**You**: **【I think I did fairly well. / Honestly, I'm not sure how it went.】** I felt a bit nervous at first, but **【once I started talking, I felt more relaxed. / after a few questions, I settled down.】**

**A**: Was there anything you found difficult?
**You**: **【The Part 3 questions were the toughest — they required deeper thinking. / I found Part 3 challenging, since they asked for opinions.】** But I tried to **【answer as clearly as I could / stay organized】**, and overall I'm **【satisfied with how it went / relieved that it's over】**.

**A**: Great, let's wait for the result.
**You**: **【Fingers crossed. Whatever the result, it's been a valuable experience. / Let's hope so. It was a good learning experience either way.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：与搭档分别扮演"考生"和"关心的朋友"，完成考前、考后两段对话；
2. **情绪转述**：用 5 个不同情绪词（nervous, anxious, relieved, satisfied, disappointed）
   各造一个完整句子，录音朗读；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否清晰描述"备考 → 考试 → 考后"的时间线？
   - 情绪表达是否有变化，而不是只会说 nervous / happy？
   - 时态是否正确（备考与考试用过去时，现状用现在时）？
4. **迁移练习**：把对话中的句型用到话题库"经历与故事"或"学业与专业"的
   Part 1 问题中，录音对比。
