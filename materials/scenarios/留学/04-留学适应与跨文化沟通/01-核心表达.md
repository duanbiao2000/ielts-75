# 留学适应与跨文化沟通 · 核心表达

## 一、描述文化差异

**客观描述**
- **【The culture here is quite different from what I'm used to. / The culture is quite different from back home.】**（这里的文化与我习惯的很不一样）
- **【I've noticed that people here … / I've found that people here …】**（我注意到这里的人…）
- **【One thing that surprised me is … / What surprised me is …】**（让我惊讶的一点是…）

**表达差异**
- **【It's not that … is wrong, but … is different. / It's not that one is wrong — they're just different.】**（并非…不对，只是…不同）
- **【I've had to adjust to … / I'm still getting used to …】**（我得适应…/仍在习惯…）

## 二、描述适应过程

**困难**
- **【At first, I found it challenging to … / In the beginning, it was difficult to …】**（起初，…对我来说很有挑战）
- **【I felt a bit homesick / out of place at first. / I felt a bit homesick and out of place at first.】**（起初我有点想家/格格不入）

**应对**
- **【What helped was … / What has helped is …】**（有帮助的是…）
- **【I kept an open mind and tried to … / I stayed open-minded and tried to …】**（我保持开放心态，努力…）
- **【By doing …, I gradually got used to it. / Through …, I gradually adapted.】**（通过…，我逐渐适应了）

**收获**
- **【Looking back, it's been a rewarding experience. / Looking back, it's been really rewarding.】**（回头看，这是一段很有收获的经历）
- **【I've become more independent / open-minded. / I've grown more independent and open-minded.】**（我变得更独立/更开放了）

## 三、跨文化沟通

**澄清误解**
- **【I think there might be a misunderstanding — what I meant was … / I think there might be a misunderstanding — what I meant is …】**（可能有误解——我想说的是…）
- **【Could I explain my point again? / Could I explain that again?】**（能让我重新解释一下我的观点吗？）

**表达尊重**
- **【I respect that, but from my perspective, … / I understand that, but from my point of view, …】**（我尊重这一点，但从我的角度…）
- **【It's interesting to see things from a different perspective. / It's interesting to see things differently.】**（从不同角度看事情很有趣）

## 四、高频句型

- I've noticed that …（我注意到…）
- At first, I found it challenging to …（起初，…对我很有挑战）
- What helped was …（有帮助的是…）
- Looking back, …（回头看，…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 描述文化差异要客观，避免"我们的更好"的评判；
- 适应过程用时间线（起初 → 后来 → 现在），层次清晰；
- 澄清误解用"先承认 + 再解释"，保持尊重。
