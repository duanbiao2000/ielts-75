# 留学适应与跨文化沟通 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：与朋友聊适应

**Friend**: How's life abroad going? Is it what you expected?

**You**: **【It's been quite an adjustment, honestly. / Honestly, it's taken some getting used to.】** **【At first, I found it challenging to fit in / to make friends】**, because **【everything felt different / the culture is quite different from back home】**.

**Friend**: What helped you adapt?

**You**: **【What helped was keeping an open mind and joining some local activities. / What really helped was staying open-minded and joining local activities.】** **【By doing that, I gradually got used to the life here / Through that, I gradually adapted】**, and **【I've met some great people along the way. / I've also made some good friends.】**

**Friend**: That's great.

**You**: **【Looking back, it's been a rewarding experience. / Looking back, it's been really rewarding.】** I've **【become more independent / grown a lot】** in the process.

## 对话二：化解误解

**Friend**: I'm a bit confused — I thought you didn't like our way of doing things.

**You**: **【I think there might be a misunderstanding — what I meant was that it's just different from what I'm used to. / I think there might be a misunderstanding — what I meant is that it's just different from what I know.】** **【It's not that one way is wrong — they're just different. / It's not that one is wrong, they're just different.】**

**Friend**: That makes sense.

**You**: **【I respect the way things are done here, but from my perspective, it took me a while to adjust. / I respect the local way of doing things, but from my point of view, it took time to adjust.】** **【It's been interesting to see things from a different perspective. / It's been interesting seeing things from a different angle.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟"聊适应"与"化解误解"，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意适应过程的时间线表达；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否客观描述文化差异，不带评判？
   - 能否清晰表达"困难 → 应对 → 收获"的过程？
   - 化解误解时能否保持尊重、清晰澄清？
4. **迁移练习**：把适应表达与话题库「移民动机与新生活」结合，
   录一段"搬到新地方的经历"的话题卡回答。
