# 校园生活服务 · 核心表达

## 一、图书馆

**借阅与查询**
- **【Could I borrow / take out】** this book?（能借这本书吗？）
- **【How long is the loan period? / How long can I keep it?】**（借期是多久？）
- **【Could you help me find …? / Could you show me where … is?】**（能帮我找…吗？）

## 二、宿舍与报修

**报修**
- **【There seems to be a problem with … / Something's wrong with …】**（…好像出了问题）
- **【Could you arrange to fix it? / Could you get it repaired?】**（能安排维修吗？）

**咨询**
- **【Could I change my room? / Is it possible to change rooms?】**（能换房间吗？）
- **【What are the rules for the dormitory? / Are there any rules I should know?】**（宿舍有什么规定？）

## 三、教务与行政

**咨询**
- **【Could you tell me how to …? / Could you explain how to …?】**（能告诉我如何…吗？）
- **【Where can I get …? / Where do I collect …?】**（在哪里能领取…？）
- **【I'd like to know more about … / I'd like to ask about …】**（我想多了解…）

**反馈**
- **【I'd like to give some feedback on … / I'd like to comment on …】**（我想对…提些反馈）
- **【Could you pass this on to the relevant department? / Could you forward this?】**（能转达给相关部门吗？）

## 四、社团与活动

- **【I'd like to join the … club. / I'd like to sign up for the … club.】**（我想加入…社团）
- **【How can I get involved in …? / How do I take part in …?】**（我如何参与…？）

## 五、高频句型

- Could I / Could you …?（能否…？）
- I'd like to …（我想…）
- Is it possible to …?（能否…？）
- I'd like to know / give feedback on …（我想了解/对…提反馈）

## 六、易错提醒

- 借书用 borrow / take out，还书用 return；
- 报修时说明具体问题与位置，便于处理；
- 反馈问题先陈述事实，再提建议，保持礼貌。
