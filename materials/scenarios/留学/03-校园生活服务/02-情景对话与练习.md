# 校园生活服务 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：图书馆咨询

**You**: Hi, **【could I borrow this book? / I'd like to borrow this book.】**

**Librarian**: Of course. **【What's your student ID? / Could I have your student ID?】**

**You**: Here you go. **【How long is the loan period? / How long can I keep it?】**

**Librarian**: **【Three weeks. / You can keep it for three weeks.】**

**You**: Thank you. **【Could you also tell me where the reference books are? / Could you show me where the reference books are kept?】**

**Librarian**: They're **【on the second floor / upstairs】**.

**You**: **【That's very helpful, thank you. / Thanks for your help.】**

## 对话二：宿舍报修

**You**: Hi, **【there seems to be a problem with the light in my room — it keeps flickering. / something's wrong with the light in my room — it keeps flickering.】** **【Could you arrange to fix it? / Could you get it repaired?】**

**Staff**: I'm sorry about that. I'll **【send someone up today / arrange a repair today】**.

**You**: Thank you. **【Also, I'd like to ask — is it possible to change rooms? / One more thing — could I possibly change rooms?】** My room is **【quite noisy / a bit noisy】** at night.

**Staff**: Let me check what's available and get back to you.

**You**: **【That's fine, thank you. / Okay, thanks a lot.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟图书馆咨询与宿舍报修，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意服务场景的礼貌与清晰；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否用多种方式咨询流程、借期、地点？
   - 报修/反馈时能否清晰描述问题与请求？
   - 能否用 Could I / Is it possible to 等礼貌句式？
4. **迁移练习**：把服务沟通表达与话题库「校园生活与教师」结合，
   录一段"我的校园生活"的回答。
