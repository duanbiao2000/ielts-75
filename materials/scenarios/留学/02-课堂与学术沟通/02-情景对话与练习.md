# 课堂与学术沟通 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：课堂发言

**Professor**: Any questions about today's topic?

**You**: **【Yes, could I ask a question about the reading? / I have a question about the reading.】** **【I'm not quite sure about the author's main argument — could you clarify it? / I'm a bit unclear about the author's main point — could you explain it?】**

**Professor**: Sure. The author is arguing that …

**You**: **【That's a good point. Building on that, I'd argue that the method has some limitations. / I see, and building on that, I'd say the method has some limitations.】** Because **【the sample size was small / the data was limited】**, the conclusion might not apply widely.

**Professor**: That's a thoughtful observation.

## 对话二：向教授咨询作业

**You**: **【Excuse me, could I discuss my essay with you? / May I talk to you about my essay?】** **【I'd like some feedback on my draft before I submit it. / I'd appreciate some feedback on my draft.】**

**Professor**: Of course. **【Send it to me in advance, and we can meet this week. / Email it to me, and we can set up a meeting.】**

**You**: Thank you. **【Do you have office hours this week? / Are you available this week?】**

**Professor**: Yes, **【Thursday at 3 p.m. / Thursday afternoon at three】**.

**You**: **【That works for me. I'll send you my draft by then. / That's fine — I'll email the draft before then.】** Thank you for your time and guidance.

**Professor**: You're welcome.

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，分别扮演学生与教授，完成课堂提问与作业咨询，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意学术场合的正式语气；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否清晰提出学术问题并表达观点？
   - 咨询作业时能否说明需求、确认时间？
   - 能否用正式礼貌的学术表达（Could I, I'd be grateful）？
4. **迁移练习**：把课堂观点表达迁移到第 5 课 Part 3 论证，
   录一段抽象话题的回答，对比结构。
