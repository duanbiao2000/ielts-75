# 课堂与学术沟通 · 核心表达

## 一、课堂发言

**提问**
- Could I ask a question about …?（我能就…问一个问题吗？）
- I'm not quite sure about … Could you **【explain / clarify】** it?（我对…不太确定，能解释一下吗？）
- **【What's your view on …? / How do you see …?】**（您对…怎么看？）

**观点表达**
- From my perspective, …（从我的角度看…）
- I'd argue that …（我认为…）
- What I find interesting is that …（我觉得有趣的是…）
- **【I see it differently — I'd say … / I have a different view — I'd say …】**（我的看法不同——我认为…）

**回应与讨论**
- That's a good point. **【Building on that, … / Following up on that, …】**（这个观点很好。在此基础上…）
- **【I'd like to add that … / Could I add that …】**（我想补充一点…）

## 二、学术咨询

**咨询作业/论文**
- **【Could I discuss my essay with you? / May I talk to you about my essay?】**
- **【I'd like some feedback on my draft. / I'd appreciate some feedback on my draft.】**
- **【Is it possible to book a meeting with you? / Could I arrange a meeting with you?】**

**预约与跟进**
- **【Do you have office hours this week? / Are you available this week?】**
- **【I'll send you my draft in advance. / I'll email you my draft beforehand.】**

## 三、学术邮件

**开头与结尾**
- Dear Professor …, / I hope this email finds you well.（尊敬的…教授，/ 希望您一切顺利）
- Thank you for your time and guidance.（感谢您的时间与指导）
- **【I look forward to hearing from you. / I'd appreciate your reply.】**

**正文**
- I'm writing to …（我写信是为了…）
- I would be grateful if you could …（如果您能…我将不胜感激）

## 四、高频句型

- Could I ask about / Could you clarify …?（我能问/您能澄清…吗？）
- From my perspective, …（从我的角度看…）
- I'd be grateful if you could …（如果您能…我将不胜感激）
- I'm writing to …（我写信是为了…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 学术场合用正式表达，提问用 Could I / Would it be possible；
- 观点表达要有依据（I'd argue that… because…）；
- 邮件注意格式：称呼、正文、致谢、署名。
