# 申请与入学注册 · 核心表达

## 一、咨询与申请

**咨询课程**
- I'd like to **【enquire about / ask about】** the **【…course / application process】**.（我想咨询…课程/申请流程）
- **【Could you tell me about the application requirements? / What are the application requirements?】**
- **【What are the entry requirements / tuition fees?】**

**提交申请**
- I'd like to **【submit my application / apply for the course】**.（我想提交申请/申请该课程）
- I've **【attached / sent】** my documents.（我已附上/发送了材料）
- **【Is there anything else I need to provide? / Anything else I need to submit?】**

**跟进**
- **【I'd like to follow up on my application. / I'm calling to follow up on my application.】**
- **【Could you tell me the status of my application? / What's the status of my application?】**
- **【When can I expect a decision? / When will I hear back?】**

## 二、入学注册

**注册确认**
- I'd like to **【register for / complete my enrolment】**.（我想注册/完成入学登记）
- **【Could you confirm my registration? / Can you confirm that my registration is complete?】**

**材料与时间**
- **【What documents do I need for registration? / What do I need to bring for registration?】**
- **【When is the registration deadline? / What's the deadline for registration?】**
- **【Could you tell me where the registration office is? / Where is the registration office?】**

## 三、正式用语

- I'm writing to / I'm calling to …（我写信/致电是为了…）
- I would be grateful if you could …（如果您能…我将不胜感激）
- Please let me know if …（如果…请告知我）
- Thank you in advance for your help.（提前感谢您的帮助）

## 四、高频句型

- I'd like to enquire about / follow up on …（我想咨询/跟进…）
- Could you tell me …?（能告诉我…吗？）
- I would appreciate it if …（如果…我将不胜感激）
- Please let me know …（请告知我…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 正式沟通用 I'd like to / Could you，避免随意口语；
- "application" 是申请，"registration / enrolment" 是注册，注意区分；
- 跟进申请时说明自己的姓名与申请编号，便于对方处理。
