# 申请与入学注册 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：咨询申请

**You**: Hello, **【I'd like to enquire about the application process for the master's programme. / I'd like to ask about applying for the master's programme.】**

**Admin**: Of course. **【What would you like to know? / What can I help you with?】**

**You**: **【Could you tell me the application requirements and deadline? / What are the requirements and the deadline?】**

**Admin**: You'll need **【a transcript, a personal statement and two references / your transcripts, a personal statement and two letters of recommendation】**. The deadline is **【31 January / January the 31st】**.

**You**: Thank you. **【I'd like to follow up on my application later — how can I check the status? / I'd like to check the status of my application later — how can I do that?】**

**Admin**: You can **【log in to the applicant portal / check the portal】** to see the status.

**You**: **【That's very helpful, thank you. / Thank you for your help.】**

## 对话二：入学注册

**You**: Hi, **【I'd like to complete my enrolment. / I'd like to register for the new term.】**

**Admin**: Sure. **【Could I have your student ID and confirm your details? / May I have your student ID?】**

**You**: Here's my ID. **【Could you confirm that my registration is complete? / Can you confirm my registration is done?】**

**Admin**: Let me check… Yes, everything is complete. **【You'll receive your timetable by email. / Your timetable will be emailed to you.】**

**You**: Perfect. **【Is there anything else I need to do for registration? / Anything else I need to do?】**

**Admin**: Just **【pay your tuition fees / settle the fees】** by the end of the month.

**You**: **【Thank you — that's very clear. / Thanks, that's very clear.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟咨询申请与入学注册，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意正式场合的礼貌用语；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否用正式表达咨询申请要求、截止日期、申请状态？
   - 注册时能否确认信息、询问材料与后续事项？
   - 能否用 I'd be grateful / Please let me know 等正式句型？
4. **迁移练习**：把申请表达与话题库「学业与专业」结合，
   录一段"我的留学/学习计划"的回答。
