# 旅行中的社交与体验 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：与当地店主聊天

**You**: Hi, **【this is my first time in this city — could you recommend a good place to eat? / it's my first visit — any recommendations for a good restaurant?】**

**Local**: Absolutely — **【there's a great local market two streets away. / there's a great local market just two streets away.】**

**You**: **【I've heard the food here is amazing — is that true? / I've heard the food is great — is that right?】**

**Local**: **【Yes, it's famous. Try the local speciality. / Definitely — you should try the local speciality.】**

**You**: **【Thank you, I'll do that. / Thanks, I'll definitely try it.】**

## 对话二：与朋友分享旅行感受

**Friend**: So, how was your trip?

**You**: **【It was fantastic — what surprised me most was how welcoming everyone was. / It was amazing — the most surprising thing was how welcoming everyone was.】** **【I was really impressed by the old town and the local culture. / I was blown away by the old town and the local culture.】**

**Friend**: Did you learn anything from the trip?

**You**: **【Yes — it broadened my horizons. / Definitely — it really broadened my horizons.】** **【I came to realise that travelling is more than just sightseeing — it's a chance to understand different ways of life. / I came to realise that travel is more than sightseeing — it's a chance to understand different ways of life.】**

**Friend**: That's a great way to put it.

**You**: **【Travelling has taught me to be more open-minded. / Travel has taught me to be more open-minded.】** **【It was a memorable experience, and I'd love to go back. / It was memorable, and I'd love to return.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟"问路咨询"与"分享旅行感受"，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，重点练感受表达与升华句；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否自然与当地人开启话题、请教建议？
   - 感受表达是否有具体对象，不空泛？
   - 能否用 broadened my horizons / taught me 等升华表达？
4. **迁移练习**：把旅行感受表达与话题库「出行与旅行」话题卡结合，
   录一段"一次难忘的旅行"的 2 分钟独白。
