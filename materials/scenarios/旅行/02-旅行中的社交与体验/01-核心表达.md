# 旅行中的社交与体验 · 核心表达

## 一、旅行交流

**寒暄与开启话题**
- **【Where are you from? / I don't think we've met — where are you from?】**（你从哪里来？）
- **【What brings you here? / What brought you here?】**（你来这里是为什么？）
- **【How's your trip going? / Is your trip going well?】**（旅行进展如何？）

**请教与分享**
- **【Could you recommend a good place to eat / visit? / Any recommendations for a good place to eat?】**
- **【I've heard that … — is it worth visiting? / I've heard about … — is it worth a visit?】**
- **【This is my first time here — any tips? / It's my first visit — any tips?】**

## 二、感受表达

**惊喜与印象**
- **【What surprised me most was … / The most surprising thing was …】**（最让我惊讶的是…）
- **【I was blown away by … / I was really impressed by …】**（…让我大为震撼）
- **【It was a memorable experience, because … / It was memorable, because …】**（这是一次难忘的经历，因为…）

**收获与感悟**
- **【It broadened my horizons / opened my eyes. / It really broadened my horizons.】**（它开阔了我的视野）
- **【I learned that … / I came to realise that …】**（我认识到/逐渐意识到…）
- **【Travelling has taught me to … / Travel has taught me to …】**（旅行教会我…）

## 三、旅行意义（可用于 Part 3）

- **【In my view, travel is more than just sightseeing. / To me, travel is more than sightseeing.】**（在我看来，旅行不只是观光）
- **【It's a chance to experience different cultures and grow as a person. / It's a chance to experience new cultures and grow.】**（它是体验不同文化、成长的机会）

## 四、高频句型

- What surprised me most was …（最让我惊讶的是…）
- I was impressed by …（我对…印象深刻）
- It broadened my horizons, because …（它开阔了我的视野，因为…）
- Travelling has taught me that …（旅行教会我…）

## 五、易错提醒

- 感受表达要有具体对象（what surprised me most was…），避免空泛；
- 与当地人交流保持好奇与尊重，用 Could you / Do you know 等礼貌表达；
- 升华感悟时用现在完成时或一般现在时，体现"影响持续至今"。
