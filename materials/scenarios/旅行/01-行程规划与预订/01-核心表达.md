# 行程规划与预订 · 核心表达

## 一、咨询与规划

**咨询行程**
- **【I'm planning a trip to … and I'd like some advice. / I'm planning a trip to … — could I ask for some advice?】**
- **【What would you recommend for a …-day trip? / What do you recommend for a …-day trip?】**
- **【Are there any must-see places in …? / What are the must-see places in …?】**

**询问路线与时间**
- **【How long does it take to get from … to …? / How long is the trip from … to …?】**
- **【What's the best time to visit …? / When is the best time to visit …?】**
- **【Is it easy to get around …? / Is it convenient to travel around …?】**

## 二、预订

**预订请求**
- **【I'd like to book a room / a ticket. / I'd like to make a booking.】**
- **【Could I reserve … for …? / Can I book … for …?】**
- **【I'd like to confirm my booking. / I'd like to confirm my reservation.】**

**确认信息**
- **【Could you confirm the details — the dates and the price? / Could you confirm the dates and price?】**
- **【Is breakfast included? / Does the price include breakfast?】**
- **【Is it possible to change the date? / Could I change the date?】**

## 三、变更与取消

- **【I'd like to change my booking to … / I'd like to modify my reservation.】**
- **【What's the cancellation policy? / What are the cancellation terms?】**
- **【Is there a fee for cancelling? / Is there a cancellation fee?】**

## 四、高频句型

- I'm planning a trip to …（我计划去…旅行）
- I'd like to book / confirm …（我想预订/确认…）
- Could you …?（能否…？）
- Is it possible to …?（能否…？）

## 五、易错提醒

- 预订用 book / reserve，确认用 confirm，变更用 change / modify，注意区分；
- 咨询行程先说明天数与偏好，对方建议更精准；
- 预订时确认日期、价格、包含项目三大细节。
