# 行程规划与预订 · 情景对话与练习

> 【】内为可替换表达。

## 对话一：咨询行程

**You**: Hi, **【I'm planning a trip to your city and I'd like some advice. / I'm planning to visit your city — could I ask for some advice?】** It's a **【three-day / three day】** trip.

**Staff**: Sure. **【What kind of trip are you planning? / What are you interested in?】**

**You**: **【I'd like to see the main attractions and try some local food. / I want to see the main sights and try local food.】** **【What would you recommend? / What do you recommend?】**

**Staff**: **【I'd recommend the old town and the riverside area. / You should definitely visit the old town and the riverside.】** And there's a famous local market for food.

**You**: **【How long does it take to get there from the centre? / How far is it from the centre?】** And **【what's the best time to visit? / when's the best time to go?】**

**Staff**: **【It's about half an hour by bus. / It's about thirty minutes by bus.】** The **【morning / early morning】** is the best time — less crowded.

**You**: **【That's very helpful, thank you. / Thanks a lot for your advice.】**

## 对话二：预订与确认

**You**: **【I'd like to book a double room for two nights. / I'd like to book a double room for two nights.】**

**Staff**: Of course. **【Could I have your name and the dates? / May I take your name and dates?】**

**You**: **【The dates are … / I'd like it for …】**. **【Could you confirm the price and whether breakfast is included? / Can you confirm the price and if breakfast is included?】**

**Staff**: It's **【… per night, and breakfast is included. / … a night, breakfast included】**.

**You**: **【That works. / That's fine.】** **【Could I confirm the booking is made? / Can you confirm the booking?】**

**Staff**: Yes, your booking is confirmed. **【You'll receive a confirmation email shortly. / A confirmation email will be sent shortly.】**

**You**: **【Thank you for your help. / Thanks, that's great.】**

## 练习任务

1. **角色扮演**：两人一组，模拟咨询行程与预订确认，角色互换；
2. **脱稿录音**：单人完成两段对话，注意咨询与预订的不同语气；
3. **自测清单**：
   - 能否用多种方式咨询行程建议？
   - 预订时能否确认日期、价格、包含项目？
   - 能否用 I'd like to book / Could I confirm 等标准预订表达？
4. **迁移练习**：把规划表达与话题库「出行与旅行」结合，
   录一段"我计划的一次旅行"的回答。
