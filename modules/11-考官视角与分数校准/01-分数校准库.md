# 分数校准库（四档示范回答）

> 用途：为模块 04 的四维度自评表提供**真实参照锚**。
> 用法：先听/读示范 → 按自评表给自己打分 → 对照注释看差距是否准确，
> 反复校准，直到自评分数与参照档位接近。
> 说明：以下为**示范性重构样例**（非官方录音），用于训练自评校准能力。

## 二、同一问题四档示范（Part 3）

**问题：Is social media good for society?**

### 档位 6.0（示范性样例）
> Social media is good for society. I think social media is very important now. People use it every day. They can talk with their friends. Also, social media can share news very fast. But sometimes social media is not good. People play games on it and waste time. So I think social media is good but people should use it carefully.

**注释（为什么是 6.0）**
- 流利度：句子短、靠简单并列，逻辑只有"好/不好"两层，衔接手段单一；
- 词汇：足够应对话题，但重复多（social media 反复原词），无搭配亮点；
- 语法：几乎全简单句，时态与结构基本正确但无复杂结构；
- 发音假设可懂。**差距点**：无转述、无复杂结构、无举例细节。

### 档位 6.5（示范性样例）
> I think social media is good for society in general, but it also has some problems. The main reason is that social media helps people keep in touch, especially when they are far away from family. Also, it's a fast way to get news and information. However, social media can be bad for young people, because they spend too much time on it and it affects their study. So I think people should use it in a proper way.

**注释（为什么是 6.5）**
- 流利度：能较长表达，有基本衔接（however, also, so），但仍显模板化；
- 词汇：有 keep in touch、in a proper way 等搭配，但转述能力有限；
- 语法：出现 because 从句等复杂结构，错误少，但结构类型仍单一；
- **差距点**：论证靠"好—坏—总结"三段式，缺举例、缺让步的细腻处理。

### 档位 7.0（示范性样例）
> I'd say social media is largely beneficial, though it does have its downsides. On the one hand, it keeps people connected across distances and allows information to spread quickly, which means people can stay informed and maintain relationships more easily than before. On the other hand, it can lead to problems such as misinformation and excessive screen time, which may affect people's wellbeing. For example, many young people find it hard to limit the time they spend on social media. So in my view, the benefits are real, but people need to manage their usage.

**注释（为什么是 7.0）**
- 流利度：长时间表达，衔接手段多样（on the one hand / on the other hand / for example / so）；
- 词汇：largely beneficial, downsides, excessive screen time 等不常见搭配，有转述；
- 语法：定语从句、非谓语、让步结构混合使用，错误偶发但不影响理解；
- **差距点**：与 7.5 的差距在**观点的细腻度与句式的自然变化**——仍偏"标准答案式"结构。

### 档位 7.5（示范性样例）
> I'd say social media is a double-edged sword, and whether it benefits society largely depends on how people and platforms use it. It certainly brings real advantages — it keeps people connected across distances, gives ordinary people a voice, and allows information, including public-service announcements, to spread faster than ever. That said, the downsides are equally genuine: misinformation can spread just as quickly, and heavy use has been linked to anxiety and poorer concentration, especially among young users. For example, many people report feeling worse about their own lives after prolonged social-media use. So in my view, social media itself is neither good nor bad; the real question is how individuals, platforms and regulators manage it, and I believe the benefits can outweigh the drawbacks when it's used with awareness.

**注释（为什么是 7.5）**
- 流利度：长时间流畅表达，让步与转折自然（that said / equally genuine），无背诵腔；
- 词汇：double-edged sword, give…a voice, public-service announcements,
  prolonged use 等地道且准确的搭配，转述自如；
- 语法：多种复杂结构自然混合（从句、被动、并列、插入语），几乎无错误；
- 发音（假设）：韵律特征丰富。**关键**：观点有层次、有具体细节，
  是"真实讨论"而非"背诵模板"。

## 三、校准练习方法

1. **自评校准**：听/读任一档示范，先遮住档位自己打分，再对照注释，
   看是否打准——连续校准 5 次后，自评偏差会显著缩小；
2. **找差距**：用自己 7.0 水平的回答对照 7.5 示范，逐句找出
   "少了哪一层"（细节？让步？转述？自然度？）；
3. **迁移**：把 7.5 示范中可自然复用的表达，用进自己的回答，
   但**整段不背**（见模块 08 反背诵警告）；
4. **记录**：在模块 04 的分数追踪中记录"自评 vs 参照档位"的偏差，
   偏差大的维度重点补。

## 四、使用纪律

- 校准库是**参照锚**，不是背诵材料——背档位示范同样会被考官识别；
- 每次模考后用本库校准一次自评分数，防止系统性高估；
- 四档对照表优先记"相邻档位的差距点"，这是提分的最短路径。
