# 高频抽象问题与示范论证

> 目标：通过完整示范，掌握"观点 → 解释 → 举例 → 让步"的论证全流程。
> 每个示范按 7.5 档标准：观点清晰、论证完整、语料地道、有让步转折。

## 一、社会与民生

### 问题 1：Is education the key to a successful career?

**示范**：I'd say education is fairly important, but it's not the only key to a successful career. The main reason is that education builds both professional knowledge and soft skills, such as critical thinking and teamwork, which employers value. For example, many successful people credit their education for the foundation it gave them. That said, some careers value hands-on experience just as much, and a few people succeed through vocational training or entrepreneurship. Still, for most people, a solid educational foundation is a crucial starting point.

### 问题 2：Should everyone have access to free healthcare?

**示范**：I'd say everyone should have access to basic healthcare. The main reason is that health is a fundamental need, and when people cannot afford care, it harms both individuals and society — preventable illnesses become more common, and productivity suffers. For example, countries with universal healthcare often have healthier populations overall. That said, free healthcare requires careful management of resources, so I believe basic care should be free, while some advanced treatments may need a reasonable contribution.

## 二、科技与媒体

### 问题 3：Will AI take over most jobs?

**示范**：I'd say AI will change many jobs, but it won't simply take them over. The main reason is that AI excels at repetitive, rule-based tasks, while many jobs rely on creativity, empathy and complex judgement, which are still human strengths. For example, roles in healthcare, education and management require human interaction that machines cannot easily replace. That said, some routine jobs will indeed disappear, so the real challenge is that people need to update their skills. In my view, AI reshapes work rather than replaces it entirely.

### 问题 4：Is social media good for society?

**示范**：I'd say social media is a double-edged sword — its impact depends on how people use it. On the one hand, it keeps people connected across distances and allows information to spread quickly, which can be a force for good. On the other hand, it can spread misinformation and create unhealthy comparison, which affects mental health. For example, many people report feeling anxious after spending too much time on social media. So in my view, social media itself is neither good nor bad; what matters is how individuals and societies manage it.

## 三、环境与发展

### 问题 5：Is it too late to protect the environment?

**示范**：I'd say it's not too late, but we need to act urgently. The main reason is that environmental problems are serious, but we already have the knowledge and technology to address them — renewable energy, waste reduction and better planning all work. For example, many cities have significantly cut pollution in a few years with the right policies. That said, time is limited, and delay makes the challenge harder. So I believe it's still possible, provided governments, companies and individuals all take action now.

### 问题 6：Should individuals be responsible for protecting the environment?

**示范**：I'd say individuals do have a responsibility, though governments and companies must also act. The main reason is that environmental problems are shaped by millions of daily choices, so individual actions add up — recycling, saving energy and choosing greener transport all contribute. For example, if everyone made small changes, the collective impact would be significant. That said, individuals alone cannot solve systemic issues like industrial pollution, which requires government regulation and corporate change. So I believe responsibility should be shared, with each side doing its part.

## 四、人文与价值观

### 问题 7：What is the key to happiness?

**示范**：I'd say the key to happiness lies more in relationships and meaning than in wealth. The main reason is that material things provide temporary satisfaction, while strong relationships and a sense of purpose bring lasting well-being. For example, studies consistently show that people with close social bonds tend to be happier overall. That said, basic financial security also matters — it's hard to be happy when basic needs are not met. So in my view, the best recipe is a balance: enough security, meaningful relationships, and a sense of purpose.

### 问题 8：Is success only about money?

**示范**：I'd say success is definitely not only about money. The main reason is that people define success in different ways — some measure it by career achievement, others by family, health or personal growth, and these things are not always connected to income. For example, many people consider a fulfilling job or a happy family to be their greatest success, even without high earnings. That said, money does provide security and options, so it is a factor. Still, I believe success is about living a life you find meaningful, not just about income.

## 五、训练方法

1. **跟读示范**：先跟读 2–3 个示范回答，体会论证节奏与语料运用；
2. **改写示范**：把示范回答用自己语言复述，再换话题仿写；
3. **独立作答**：遮住示范，对同一问题独立回答，录音对比差距；
4. **迁移运用**：把示范中的论证句用到其他问题，做到举一反三。
