# 抽象议题分类与论证句

> 目标：掌握抽象议题的"通用弹药"——观点句、理由句、论证句，
> 任何抽象问题都能快速调用，做到"有观点、有理由、有论证"。

## 一、四大类抽象议题

### 1. 社会与民生（教育、医疗、住房、就业、贫困）
### 2. 科技与媒体（互联网、AI、社交媒体、信息安全）
### 3. 环境与发展（污染、气候、城市化、可持续发展）
### 4. 人文与价值观（幸福、成功、文化、人际关系、道德）

## 二、通用论证句（跨议题，优先掌握）

### 观点句
- I'd say that … / From my point of view, …
- It seems to me that … / As far as I'm concerned, …
- It depends on … / It varies from person to person.（折中）

### 理由句
- The main reason is that … / This is because …
- …, which means … / …, as a result, …
- What matters most is … / It's mainly due to …

### 举例句
- For example, … / Take … for instance.
- A good case in point is … / For instance, many people …

### 让步句
- That's true, but … / On the other hand, …
- Although …, I still believe that … / That said, …

### 总结句
- So, to sum up, … / Overall, …
- In my view, … / All in all, …

## 三、分议题语料

### 议题 1：社会与民生

**观点句**
- Education is the foundation of a society's development.（教育是社会发展的基础）
- Access to healthcare should be available to everyone.（医疗资源应人人可及）
- Housing affordability is a growing concern in cities.（城市住房可负担性日益受关注）

**理由句**
- The main reason is that it affects people's quality of life.（主要原因是它影响生活质量）
- It's largely due to rapid urbanisation and population growth.（很大程度上源于快速城市化与人口增长）

**论证句**
- For example, many families struggle to afford decent housing.（例如，许多家庭难以负担体面住房）
- That said, government action can make a real difference.（不过，政府行动能产生实质影响）

### 议题 2：科技与媒体

**观点句**
- Technology is a double-edged sword — it depends on how we use it.（科技是双刃剑，取决于如何使用）
- Social media has reshaped how people communicate.（社交媒体重塑了人们的沟通方式）
- AI will fundamentally change the job market.（AI 将根本性改变就业市场）

**理由句**
- The main reason is that it brings both convenience and new challenges.（主要因为它带来便利与新挑战）
- This is because information spreads faster than ever.（这是因为信息传播比以往更快）

**论证句**
- For example, while social media connects people, it can also cause anxiety.（例如，社交媒体连接人们，也可能导致焦虑）
- That said, with proper regulation, its benefits can outweigh the drawbacks.（不过，合理监管下，其利大于弊）

### 议题 3：环境与发展

**观点句**
- Environmental protection requires a joint effort from everyone.（环保需要每个人的共同努力）
- Sustainable development is the only way forward.（可持续发展是唯一出路）
- Individual actions matter, even if they seem small.（个人行动很重要，即使看似微小）

**理由句**
- The main reason is that our actions collectively shape the environment.（主要原因是我们的行为共同塑造环境）
- It's largely due to the way modern society consumes resources.（很大程度上源于现代社会的消费方式）

**论证句**
- For example, simple habits like recycling add up to a big impact.（例如，回收这样的简单习惯累积起来影响巨大）
- Although progress is slow, every small step counts.（虽然进展缓慢，但每一步都有意义）

### 议题 4：人文与价值观

**观点句**
- True success comes from doing what you find meaningful.（真正的成功来自做有意义的事）
- Happiness is more about relationships than material things.（幸福更多关乎关系而非物质）
- Culture shapes how we see the world.（文化塑造我们的世界观）

**理由句**
- The main reason is that … brings long-term satisfaction, not just short-term pleasure.
  （主要原因是…带来长期满足，而非短暂快乐）
- This is because people's values guide their choices.（这是因为人的价值观引导其选择）

**论证句**
- For example, people who have strong social bonds tend to be happier.（例如，拥有紧密社会关系的人往往更幸福）
- That said, what defines success varies from person to person.（不过，成功的定义因人而异）

## 四、使用纪律

1. **先背通用论证句**（观点-理由-举例-让步），这是所有议题的骨架；
2. **再按议题背 3–5 个观点句**，做到有话可说；
3. **每次回答刻意用 2–3 个新语料**，形成主动调用习惯；
4. **进 Anki**：观点句与论证句按议题分类入库，间隔复习。
